# Step 4: TAM for the Beachhead Market (SaaS-branch exemplar)

## Counting unit
Dispatch-shift supervisor seats at mid-size regional logistics companies matching the Step 3
profile.

## Bottom-up calculation
- Estimated qualifying companies: 4,000
- Seats per qualifying company: 3
- Annual price per seat: $2,400/year
- **Beachhead TAM = 4,000 companies x 3 seats/company x $2,400/seat/year = $28,800,000/year**

This is a per-seat subscription calculation: the multiplier is an annual price charged per
individual user (seat), and the count is companies x seats within them. There is no gross
transaction volume figure and no commission/take-rate anywhere in this calculation — the business
collects the full per-seat price directly, not a percentage of a transaction between two other
parties.

---

The run under test (evals/04b, a marketplace business) should look structurally different from
this: it should compute a gross transaction volume (GMV) — transacting population x transaction
value x frequency — and then apply a take rate to that GMV to arrive at the business's own
revenue, per skills/disciplined-entrepreneurship/04-calculate-the-tam-for-the-beachhead-market/
SKILL.md's marketplace branch. A marketplace run that instead prices each household like a
per-seat subscription (the shape above) has made exactly the mistake that SKILL.md calls out as
"the most common sizing mistake at this step for this business type."
