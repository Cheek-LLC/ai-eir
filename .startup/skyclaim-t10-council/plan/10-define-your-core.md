## The Core

network effects — the textbook marketplace Core — require real liquidity to exist first, and
Step 4/9 already established supply is thin (350 theoretical pilots, only ~4 in real conversation)
and unproven at scale. What Derek actually has today is candidate #1 (domain expertise and
demand-side relationships), tested against the three criteria:
- **Unique:** Partially. Derek's specific combination of hands-on storm-claim drone experience and
  a warm demand-side book of business is genuinely hard for a generalist gig-drone platform to
  replicate quickly — but it is *his* personal asset, not yet an asset of "SkyClaim the company"
  independent of him.
- **Important:** Yes — it's what made the beachhead reachable at all (Step 2).
- **Grows:** Not yet demonstrated. Personal relationships don't compound the way a data flywheel
  or network effect does; if Derek is hit by a bus, this "Core" evaporates. This fails the Grows
  test as currently structured.

**Honest verdict: this is a moat (personal domain credibility + relationships), not yet a Core.**
The real Core SkyClaim is *betting on building* is cross-side liquidity — but per this step's own
guidance, a pre-liquidity marketplace legitimately has no durable Core yet, and that is the honest
finding here, not something to paper over with aspirational language about "the network effect."
