# Step 5: Profile the Persona for the Beachhead Market

## Which persona this is

Per this step's services guidance, naming explicitly: this profile is **Profile A from Step 3 —
the hiring CTO/technical co-founder ("Dana")**, the person who experiences the problem day to day
(a stalled, high-stakes leadership hire eating her own bandwidth), not the person who commissions
the engagement (Profile B, the Head of Talent/CEO, who is a real and distinct decision-maker
covered in Step 12's DMU, not re-profiled here to avoid duplicating Step 3).

## A day in Dana's life, around this problem

Dana runs a ~35-person engineering org inside a ~130-person company. Her calendar this week: two
board-prep meetings, a production incident retro, 6 hours of 1:1s, and — squeezed between all of
that — 90 minutes she'd blocked for candidate sourcing that got bumped twice by the incident. She
has an open VP Engineering req that's been live for 11 weeks. She's personally screened 40+
LinkedIn profiles and gotten 4 first-round conversations from it, none of whom cleared her bar on
architectural judgment at the scale her company is about to hit (they're strong ICs promoted into
management, not people who've actually run a 35-person org through a scaling inflection point
before). She is not struggling to *evaluate* candidates — she's struggling to *find* candidates
worth evaluating, on top of an already full job.

## Goals

- Fill the VP Engineering seat with someone who can run the org at 2x its current size within 18
  months, not just today's size.
- Get her own time back — every week this stays open is a week she's doing two jobs.
- Avoid a bad hire more than she wants a fast hire — she has already made one too-fast promotion
  mistake at a prior company and is explicit about not repeating it.

## Frustrations

- Her own network is exhausted — she's called in every favor she has.
- Generalist recruiters send her strong-on-paper candidates who can't survive a real systems-
  design conversation.
- She doesn't have bandwidth to run a proper search process herself on top of her actual job.

## Open assumptions

None new — built directly from Step 3's evidence base.
