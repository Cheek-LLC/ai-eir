## Channel gap, stated plainly (per this skill's own instruction)

`plan/02-select-a-beachhead-market.md` does not name a specific channel where this beachhead
spends attention (no trade publication, community, or event named). In the absence of that, this
calendar uses the most defensible partial evidence available rather than defaulting silently to
generic social:

- **LinkedIn** — Step 5's persona detail states Dana personally "screened 40+ LinkedIn profiles"
  as part of her own DIY search; Step 9's real prospect list shows 2 of Jordan's own two direct
  (non-referral) engagements came from his own LinkedIn outreach, and one inbound prospect (#8)
  found Jordan via a placed candidate's LinkedIn post. This is real, if partial, evidence that
  LinkedIn is where this persona is reachable — used as the primary channel.
- **Direct relationship touches (email/1:1), not broadcast content** — Step 9's access-path note
  states explicitly that 6 of 8 real signed engagements came from referrals (past candidates now
  hiring managers, past clients referring a portfolio peer), not from broadcast content at all.
  This is the single most load-bearing channel this business actually has, and it is *not* a
  content-calendar-shaped channel — it's covered by `gtm/outbound-sales-playbook.md`'s relationship-
  maintenance touches, not duplicated here. Flagging this explicitly so the calendar below isn't
  read as "here's how Vantage Point Search actually gets clients" — it's the awareness/credibility
  layer that supports the referral engine, not a replacement for it.

## 3. Launch-day sequence (2026-09-01)

Order matters — warmest audience first:

1. **Morning — direct personal email** to Jordan's existing warm network (past clients, past
   candidates now in hiring-manager roles, the 2 VC talent partners named in Step 9's referral
   sources): short, personal note that Vantage Point Search is actively taking on new engagements,
   using the **10-second elevator pitch** from `positioning.md`. This is the highest-intent
   audience and goes out before any public post.
   - **Connector status:** sending this at scale (beyond Jordan's personal inbox) would normally
     go through an email connector — see `gtm/launch-plan.md` §readiness for the connectors-liaison
     status on this. For launch day specifically, Jordan is sending this personally from his own
     inbox to a short, known list (not a bulk send), which does not require a connector.
2. **Midday — LinkedIn launch post**, using the **30-second elevator pitch**, tagged to reach the
   broader network including weak ties (past colleagues, conference contacts).
3. **Afternoon — 1:1 follow-up messages** to the 3 next-10 prospects currently in "first
   conversation" or "discovery call scheduled" status (Step 9 #1, #4, #7), referencing the launch
   post directly ("just published a longer piece on exactly the problem we talked about — thought
   you'd want to see it") — this is the calendar's one deliberate tie-in to
   `gtm/outbound-sales-playbook.md`'s sequence, so content and outbound reinforce each other rather
   than running as separate motions.

## 4. Ongoing cadence template (post-6-week window)

A repeatable weekly template for Jordan (or `marketing-strategist`, if re-invoked) to execute past
this initial push:

- **1x technical-depth post/week** — a real screening question, a real interview-loop insight, or
  a competitive-differentiation post (Pillar 1).
- **1x founder-voice post every other week** — market observations, process transparency, or a
  "here's a search I turned down and why" post (keeps the practice's actual selectivity visible,
  which itself supports the credibility pillar).
- **1x track-record post/month** — a new anonymized placement story as they happen, not manufactured
  on a fixed schedule; skip the month if there's no new real placement to report rather than
  padding with a repeat story.
- **Direct relationship touches** — not on this calendar; owned by the outbound playbook's ongoing
  relationship-maintenance cadence (Step 18's ~29 hrs/engagement background-maintenance time),
  reinforced by content, not replaced by it.

This is a template, not itself a dated schedule — Jordan (or whoever executes GTM going forward)
picks specific dates and topics against it each week.
