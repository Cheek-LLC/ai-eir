# Step 7: High-Level Product Specification

## What's actually being delivered

Not software — a bounded service engagement. The "product spec" for a retained search is the
scope of what the client is buying:

1. A scoped search brief, agreed with the client (role definition, comp band, must-have/nice-to-
   have criteria, target company list for sourcing).
2. Active sourcing and outbound engagement against that brief (target: 40-60 real candidate
   conversations per search, drawn from Jordan's network plus targeted outbound).
3. A screened slate of 4-6 candidates within ~30 days, each with Jordan's own technical/leadership
   assessment written up, not just a resume forward.
4. Interview-process coordination and candidate-experience management through to offer.
5. Offer-negotiation support and a 90-day placement guarantee.

## What's explicitly NOT included

- Onboarding the hire post-start (Jordan is not an HR/onboarding vendor).
- Guaranteed fill within a fixed timeframe (the guarantee is a replacement-search guarantee, not a
  time-to-fill SLA — average is ~11 weeks, but this is not contractually promised).
- Volume/multiple-simultaneous-role discounting at this stage — each engagement is priced and
  scoped independently (see Step 16).

## Traceability to Step 6

This spec traces directly to the observed engagement life cycle in Step 6 — it was not built first
and justified after the fact; the slate-size, screening depth, and guarantee terms all come from
what Jordan's 6 real engagements actually delivered, not an aspirational feature list.
