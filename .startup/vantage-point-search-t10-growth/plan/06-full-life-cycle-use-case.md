# Step 6: Full Life Cycle Use Case

## Before Vantage Point Search (observed, not imagined)

Dana (Step 5) tries to fill the VP Engineering role herself: posts internally on LinkedIn, asks
her network, maybe engages a contingency recruiter who sends unqualified candidates because they
can't screen for the technical bar. 10-14 weeks in, she's burned significant personal time, the
org is feeling the leadership gap, and she still doesn't have a hire. This sequence is directly
observed across all 6 closed engagements — every one of Jordan's real clients tried something else
first and it didn't work, which is exactly why they called him.

## Full life cycle with Vantage Point Search

1. **Trigger** — the role has been open long enough (usually 10+ weeks of Dana's own attempt)
   that the cost of continuing to DIY it exceeds the cost of a retained fee.
2. **Discovery** — Jordan and Dana (and often the Head of Talent, per Step 12's DMU) scope the
   role: what "VP Engineering who can run this at 2x scale" actually means concretely, comp band,
   must-haves vs. nice-to-haves.
3. **Engagement signed** — retainer contract, 1/3 fee paid upfront.
4. **Search execution** — Jordan sources, screens, and presents a slate (typically 4-6 candidates)
   within ~30 days.
5. **Client process** — Dana and her team run their own interview loop on Jordan's slate; Jordan
   coordinates scheduling and manages candidate experience/expectations throughout (a real,
   time-consuming part of the engagement that clients rarely see directly).
6. **Offer and close** — Jordan supports offer negotiation (comp benchmarking, counter-offer
   management); candidate accepts, start date set.
7. **Guarantee window** — 90 days post-start; if the hire leaves, Jordan re-runs the search at no
   additional fee.
8. **After** — the role is filled, Dana gets her bandwidth back, and (per Step 9) this relationship
   is the single most common source of Jordan's next signed engagement, whether a repeat search at
   the same company or a referral to another portfolio company.

## Open assumptions

None new here — directly observed across real engagements, not constructed from first principles.
