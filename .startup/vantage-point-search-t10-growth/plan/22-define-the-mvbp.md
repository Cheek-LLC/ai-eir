# Step 22: Define the Minimum Viable Business Product (MVBP)

## Two distinct MVBP questions here, both addressed explicitly

Per this step's services guidance, a services MVBP is usually close to the eventual delivery model
already — confirmed directly: Vantage Point Search's core offer (one retained search, real price,
real delivery) **already cleared this bar 6 times** over the last 14 months (`venture_stage:
already_operating`). Retrospectively confirming this rather than treating MVBP as a hypothetical
still to be run:

### MVBP #1 (already validated, retrospectively) — the core offer itself

1. **The offer:** one retained search engagement, exactly as specified in Step 7 — scoped brief,
   40-60 real candidate conversations, a 4-6 candidate screened slate within ~30 days, interview
   coordination, offer support, 90-day guarantee.
2. **The price:** $70,500 average (real, from Step 16 — no "founding client discount").
3. **The delivery process:** entirely manual/concierge — Jordan personally sources, screens, and
   coordinates every step. No automation anywhere in the delivery chain currently.
4. **Sales motion:** the real 5-stage process from Step 18.
5. **What it proves:** already proven — a real paying client will pay full retained-search fee for
   this exact scope, 6 times over, with zero of the 90-day guarantees invoked (no early
   departures).
6. **Definition of success:** already met and exceeded — this MVBP's own bar (get one real client
   to pay and receive value) was cleared on the first engagement; the business has since repeated
   it 5 more times.

**Can the founder actually deliver this MVBP manually at this price, without losing money or
blowing through available hours?** Yes, per Step 19's numbers: ~90 hours delivery + ~45 hours BD
per engagement (135 hours total) against a $70,500 fee clears comfortably, and Jordan has
physically done this 6 times without missing a delivery. The arithmetic does not depend on
Jordan's time being valued at zero — see Step 19's explicit market-rate check.

### MVBP #2 (not yet validated) — the next real test, tied directly to ka-020-associate-replication

The forward-looking MVBP-shaped question this plan actually needs answered next is different: **can
someone other than Jordan deliver this same offer at this same price without losing money or
missing the bar?** This has never been tested. Per Step 21's test design: hire one associate
recruiter, run their first search as a real paid engagement at full price, with Jordan doing
oversight/QA only. This is explicitly *not* a free diagnostic call or an internal training
exercise — it is a real paying client, exactly per this step's own warning against letting "MVBP"
collapse into something unpaid.

## What's explicitly excluded from MVBP #2, and why that's acceptable

The associate's first search will be run with Jordan actively available for real-time QA
(reviewing candidate write-ups before they go to the client) — full independence (Jordan not
involved at all) is explicitly deferred to a later test, not conflated with this one. This is a
deliberate, named scope boundary, not scope creep hidden as minimalism.

## Definition of success (MVBP #2)

The associate-led search reaches a signed placement within a comparable timeframe to Jordan's own
historical average (11 weeks), with a client-satisfaction signal comparable to Jordan's own
engagements (no material complaint about candidate quality), **and** Jordan's own QA time on that
search stays under 15 hours (i.e., meaningfully less than the ~90 hours he'd spend delivering it
himself) — otherwise the "associate frees up founder capacity" premise the whole growth plan rests
on isn't actually true yet.

## Open assumptions

Directly restates `ka-020-associate-replication` (Step 20/21) as this step's central open
question — not duplicating the entry, just confirming it's the one this step's design targets.
