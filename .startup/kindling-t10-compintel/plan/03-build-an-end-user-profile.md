# Step 3: End User Profile

**Note on business-type branching:** this step's SKILL.md branching section lists SaaS, Physical
product, Marketplace, Services — no `consumer_app` bullet. Used the generic profile-dimension
template as-is (firmographic/demographic anchor, behavioral, psychographic, distinguishing trait)
since nothing consumer-app-specific was offered; the questions turned out to translate reasonably
well without a dedicated branch, unlike Steps 2 and 4 below. Flagged for the QA record.

## Research basis

14 real conversations/observations — the informal 6-week pilot cohort (WhatsApp group + shared
spreadsheet, Jan 6–Feb 16, 2026), all real people from Priya's two sketch-meetup groups. This is
real primary research, but it is entirely drawn from the founder's existing network (see Step 9's
signal check and the confirmation-bias note logged here) — no cold-outreach conversations yet.

## Profile dimensions

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| Firmographic/demographic anchor | Adults 24-42 (pilot cohort skewed 26-38), employed full-time, moderate discretionary income, own a tablet (iPad + Apple Pencil, or similar) or a physical sketchbook — not art students, not professionals |
| Behavioral characteristics | Has tried at least one self-directed "30-day drawing challenge" (Instagram-style) before and abandoned it, usually within 10-20 days; posts occasionally to Instagram but doesn't currently belong to a structured accountability group; moderate tech comfort, downloads new apps readily |
| Psychographic characteristics | Values creative expression as identity/hobby, not career; motivated by consistency and visible progress more than technical mastery; mildly self-critical about "not being good enough," which the pilot showed drives quitting when unwitnessed |
| Distinguishing/trigger characteristic | Has *already* attempted and abandoned a solo streak in the last 12 months (not someone starting from zero interest) — this recency and specificity is what separates a real prospect from a generic "likes art" person |

## Narrative profile

"This person picked up sketching again during a slow period — a New Year's resolution, a
influencer-run 30-day challenge, a friend's suggestion — posted for a week or two on Instagram,
felt good about it, then missed a day, felt behind, and quietly stopped. They still think of
themselves as 'someone who wants to draw more' a year later. They struggle specifically with
*isolation* in the habit — nobody notices when they stop, so there's no friction against quitting."

## In-profile vs. out-of-profile

**In profile:** has tried and dropped a self-directed creative streak in the last 12 months, is
still socially connected to a creative-hobbyist community (even loosely), owns the tools already.
**Out of profile (looks similar, isn't):** professional/working artists (different need — income
and portfolio, not habit-formation); complete beginners who've never attempted a streak (different
problem — skill acquisition, not accountability); people who already maintain a solo streak
successfully (no pain to solve).

## Relationship to the economic buyer

Same person — this is a self-serve consumer subscription; the end user pays for their own account.
