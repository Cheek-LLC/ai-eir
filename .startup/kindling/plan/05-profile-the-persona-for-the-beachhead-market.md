# Step 5: Persona for the Beachhead Market

Business-type branching used: **Consumer app** — centered the day-in-the-life on the
attention/habit moment (not a workday), what's already on her home screen, and who she'd tell.
This branch is present, specific, and easy to draft against — a sharp contrast to Steps 2 and 4.

## Persona snapshot

**Maya Torres**, 29, marketing coordinator at a mid-size logistics company, lives alone in
Sacramento. A real member of the pilot cohort (name changed at her request; everything else is
accurate to what she actually said/did during the 6-week pilot).

## Day in the life

Maya downloaded a "30-day sketch challenge" app-store list in early January after seeing an
Instagram reel. She sketched every day for 9 days, posted a few to her own Instagram, got a
handful of likes from friends who don't draw, and felt good. On day 10 she had a late work call,
skipped it, told herself she'd catch up tomorrow — and didn't open her sketchbook again for three
weeks. Nobody asked where she went. She still has the sketchbook on her nightstand. In the pilot,
the moment that mattered was **10:30pm, phone in hand, scrolling**, deciding between one more
TikTok video and opening the WhatsApp group to see whether anyone else had posted yet — the group
pressure (in a good way) is what got her to actually draw most nights, not a calendar reminder.

## Goals & how they're measured

Not measured by anyone externally — this is entirely self-directed. Her real goal, in her own
words during the pilot debrief: "I just want to be someone who actually does this, not someone who
says they're going to."

## Frustrations & current workaround

Frustration: "I don't have anyone in my day-to-day life who'd notice or care if I stopped." Current
workaround before the pilot: posting to personal Instagram, which produces likes but no actual
accountability — nobody follows up when she goes quiet.

## Decision-making style & trusted sources

Discovers new apps/habits from Instagram Reels and TikTok, and — more durably — from friends in
her two art-adjacent group chats. Trusts peer recommendation over app-store ratings or ads; she
said explicitly she'd never have joined a generic "streak app" from an ad, but joined the pilot
because a friend personally invited her.

## Representative quote

"The sketchbook thing isn't hard because drawing is hard. It's hard because it's so easy to just...
not, when literally nothing happens if I don't."

## Grounding

Real interviewee — one of the 14 pilot participants, one of the 9 who completed the full 6 weeks.

## Assumptions flagged

None — this persona is grounded in a real, named (pseudonymized) individual from the pilot, not a
hypothesis.
