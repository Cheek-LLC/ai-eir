# Step 1: Market Segmentation

## Segmentation approach

Segmented by the two axes that actually change how a search is sold and delivered for a solo
retained-search consultant: (a) the client company's stage/size (which drives budget authority,
fee tolerance, and urgency) and (b) the role being searched (which drives sourcing difficulty,
required domain fluency, and competitive intensity from other search providers). Brainstormed
from Jordan's own 14 months of real inbound/referral conversations, not a generic industry list.

## Candidate segments

| # | Segment | Company profile | Role searched | Why distinct |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VC-backed Series B-D VP/Dir Engineering | 50-500 employees, $15M-$150M raised | VP/Director of Engineering | Jordan's own prior in-house recruiting experience is at exactly this stage band; underserved (too small for big search firms, too senior for generalist contingency recruiters) |
| 2 | VC-backed Series A VP/Dir Engineering | <50 employees, <$15M raised | VP/Director of Engineering | Smaller budgets, frequently resist a 30%-of-base retained fee; often try to hire via founder network first |
| 3 | VC-backed Series B-D VP/Dir Product | 50-500 employees | VP/Director of Product | Adjacent role, same company profile, different sourcing network (product leaders, not engineering leaders) |
| 4 | VC-backed Series B-D VP/Dir Data/ML | 50-500 employees | VP/Director of Data or ML | Adjacent role, growing category post-2023 AI hiring wave, smaller talent pool |
| 5 | Growth-stage (Series D+) C-level (CTO/CPO) | 300+ employees | CTO, CPO | Higher fee per search ($150K+), but Jordan competes directly with established firms (Heidrick, Spencer Stuart) who have brand and multi-recruiter benches he lacks |
| 6 | Engineering manager / senior IC | Any VC-backed startup | Eng Manager, Staff Engineer | Much higher volume, but contingency-fee market (10-15%, not retained), race-to-fill against 5+ competing recruiters per role |
| 7 | Non-tech-industry VP Eng (fintech/healthtech infra) | 50-500 employees, regulated vertical | VP/Director Engineering | Same role shape, added domain-compliance sourcing constraint (security clearance, HIPAA-adjacent experience) |

## Segment notes

Segments defined around real, independently-observable client characteristics (funding stage,
headcount band, role level) that existed before Jordan's business did — not around a feature of
his own service. Segment 1 (the eventual beachhead, selected in Step 2) is where 6 of Jordan's 8
signed engagements to date actually came from, which is itself evidence this segment is real, not
a hopeful guess.
