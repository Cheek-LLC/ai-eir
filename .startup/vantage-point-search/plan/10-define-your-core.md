# Step 10: Define Your Core

## The core

Jordan's core is **his personal network plus a demonstrated, repeatable technical-screening
method** — specifically: (1) 11 years of relationships with engineering leaders and candidates at
exactly the beachhead's company profile, and (2) a structured technical-assessment rubric he
developed in-house and now applies consistently across searches (not formalized as written IP yet
— see `ka-010-playbook-informal` below).

## Honest assessment: is this a durable core, or just "I execute well"?

This is closer to founder-market fit and execution quality than a classic defensible moat (no
network effects, no proprietary data asset, no patent). It is real and currently working — but per
this step's own discipline, it should be named honestly rather than dressed up as more defensible
than it is. The single biggest risk to the core as currently defined: **it lives entirely in
Jordan's head and relationships.** If Jordan is unavailable, the core doesn't transfer. This is
the same capacity/key-person thread that runs through Steps 15/17/19/22/24 — flagged here at its
root rather than only downstream.

## What would make this a more durable core

Codifying the technical-screening rubric into a written, teachable playbook (already flagged as a
roadmap dependency in Step 15) is the single highest-leverage move to convert "Jordan's personal
skill" into "Vantage Point Search's institutional method" — a precondition for the associate-
recruiter hiring plan in Step 24, not just a nice-to-have.
