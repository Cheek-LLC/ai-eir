# Step 13: Map the Process to Acquire a Paying Customer

## Stages (qualitative — DMU-keyed; costed rigor added in Step 18)

1. **First real conversation** — referral intro or Jordan's own outbound; involves the champion
   (Dana-type) most often, sometimes the economic buyer directly at smaller companies.
2. **Discovery call** — scoping the role with the champion; economic buyer sometimes present,
   sometimes briefed after.
3. **Proposal sent** — fee structure, timeline, guarantee terms; goes to the economic buyer.
4. **Internal budget check** — at larger/later-stage clients, the economic buyer takes the
   proposal to a comp committee/board member or CFO before signing (Step 12's third DMU role).
5. **Engagement signed & first installment paid** — the economic buyer signs; this is where the
   acquisition process ends and delivery (Step 22/24) begins.

## Real conversion data across all 10 tracked real conversations (Step 9) plus the 8 historical
signed engagements

- Stage 1 → Stage 2 (real conversation → discovery call): ~74% (26 of ~35 real conversations over
  14 months advanced to a discovery call — founder-tracked count, not a CRM export).
- Stage 2 → Stage 3 (discovery call → proposal sent): ~69% (18 of 26).
- Stage 3 → Stage 5 (proposal → signed): ~44% (8 of 18) — the budget-check stage (4) is where most
  of this drop happens, especially at larger clients.
- Overall conversation-to-signed: ~23% (8 of 35).
