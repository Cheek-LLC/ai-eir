# Step 5: Persona for the Beachhead Market

## Persona snapshot
Jordan Vasquez — General Manager, flagship location of a 14-unit chicken-sandwich QSR franchise
group, Fort Worth, TX. (Real name used with her permission, per Maria's note during onboarding.)

## Day in the life
Jordan opens at 6:00am. At 6:45am, one of two scheduled grill cooks texts that he's sick. She's
already running the register herself because the group is short-staffed on a normal day. She
pulls the laminated call list taped inside the office door and starts working down it in
seniority order — six names. Two don't answer. One says no. By 7:40am, with the breakfast rush
already backing up, she gets a "maybe, give me 20 minutes" from the fourth name, and ends up
also texting a closing-shift employee to see if they can come in early. The actual fix — someone
physically at the grill — doesn't happen until roughly 7:30-8:00am, 45-75 minutes after the
original call-out. This sequence, in some variation, happens at her location about 3 times a
week.

## Goals & how they're measured
Two hard numbers corporate tracks on her: labor cost as a percentage of sales (target ≤28%), and
on-time-opening compliance (audited; a late or degraded opening is a visible mark against her).
She is not measured on anything related to how she solves staffing gaps — only on whether the
symptoms (cost, lateness) show up.

## Frustrations & current workaround
Current workaround: a laminated call list in seniority order, plus a 40-person group text thread
that mostly gets ignored during work hours. She has no visibility into who else across the
group's other 13 locations might be free and willing to pick up a shift — even though several
sister locations are a 10-15 minute drive away.

## Decision-making style & trusted sources
She does not buy software — her Director of Operations decides what tools the group uses, and
Jordan is a mandated user, not a chooser. She trusts other GMs in a private Facebook group tied to
the franchise brand for informal advice, more than anything corporate sends down.

## Representative quote
"By the time I get through my call list it's already a crisis, not a fix."
